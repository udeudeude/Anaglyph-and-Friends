Standalone Mac edition. Includes Python, the built web interface, local rendering
and the verified Depth Anything V2 Small model. No Terminal setup or separate
Python/Node/model installation. Processing stays on your Mac and works offline.

Stereo synthesis now uses subpixel shifts and background-aware gap repair.
Choose **Set screen depth…**, then click a subject in the original photo to place
it at the screen plane. **Show repaired areas** marks reconstructed pixels in
orange in the main stereo previews; saved outputs stay clean. No extra AI model
or GPU is required. Hidden background texture remains an approximation.

Download the Intel or Apple Silicon ZIP, unzip, move the app to Applications,
then double-click. Keep the control window open while working in your browser;
use its Quit button when finished.

**Not Apple-notarized:** if blocked, use System Settings → Privacy & Security →
Open Anyway after attempting to open it. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

Cloud-tested on macOS 15 Intel and macOS 14 Apple Silicon. Older macOS versions
are unverified. Finder/Gatekeeper behavior on a downloaded copy needs user testing.
SHA-256 files are provided beside the ZIPs. This package does not replace the
existing developer launchers or the lightweight hosted Render edition.
