ANAGLYPH & FRIENDS — STANDALONE MAC EDITION

Choose Intel for a Mac with an Intel processor; choose Apple Silicon for M1/M2/M3
and later Apple chips. Apple menu > About This Mac identifies yours.

Unzip. Drag Anaglyph & Friends.app into Applications. Double-click it.
Its small control window opens your local workspace in your normal browser.
Keep that window open while working; its Quit button stops the local service.
No Terminal, Python, Node, Git checkout or separate model installation is needed.
The bundled V2 Small AI model works offline. V3 browser AI remains in the hosted
web edition, not this first standalone local package. Images stay on this Mac.

These downloads are NOT Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. If macOS blocks
the app, use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after attempting
to open it. On older macOS, Control-click > Open may be available. Do not disable
Gatekeeper globally. See https://support.apple.com/102445 .

Cloud tested on macOS 15 (Intel) and macOS 14 (Apple Silicon). Older macOS versions
are not yet verified; these downloads do not promise compatibility with them.
Logs: ~/Library/Logs/Anaglyph-and-Friends/desktop.log
Working files: ~/Library/Application Support/Anaglyph-and-Friends/Desktop/
Closing the browser does not quit the app; use Quit in the control window.
